Salah is one of the five pillars of Islam. Muslims are required to pray five times every day. These prayers are Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha. Salah helps Muslims remember Allah, build discipline, and stay connected to their faith throughout the day.

Patience is also an important quality in Islam. Muslims are encouraged to remain patient during hardship and trust Allah. Patience helps a believer stay strong during difficulties and avoid hopelessness.

Charity is highly encouraged in Islam. Giving to the poor and helping those in need purifies wealth and brings society closer together. A Muslim should give sincerely for the sake of Allah.
